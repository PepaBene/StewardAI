# Methods

How the insight features are calculated, with the thresholds they use and the tests that pin them.

- dividend_income: forward 12-month dividend schedule, with declared, estimated and unconfirmed rows
- checks: the five scored check axes (value, future, past, health, dividend) and the risk checks
- portfolio_scorecard: the value-weighted mean of the holdings' check scores per axis
- multiple_path: valuation from projected revenue, margin and an exit P/E, discounted to today
- fair_multiple: an indicative P/E from a peer regression on growth and margin
- risk_score: one 0 to 100 risk score from volatility, drawdown, shortfall, concentration and beta
- screen_draft: how a plain-English request becomes screener filters, and how that is evaluated
- realised_unrealised: the split of price gains into realised and unrealised, and closed positions with an annual rate
